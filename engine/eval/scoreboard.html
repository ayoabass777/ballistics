Consensus Tiers — The Key Insight

When multiple models independently agree on a pick, accuracy jumps dramatically. Solo picks (1 model) are 5.4% overconfident. Duo picks (2 models) are perfectly calibrated. Trio+ picks (3-4 models) are slightly underconfident — the sweet spot.

Market Calibration Tiers

Over 1.5 tiers are calibrated from DC + Poisson consensus thresholds. Under 3.5 tiers here are probability-only; the consensus slip applies the extra attack/defence strength gate before treating them as playable.
