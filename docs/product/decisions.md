- Strength: Big dog win rate for its gap band (250–300, 300–400, 400+; overall group rate if the band has < 100 matches) minus the league win rate at the favourite's venue, falling back to the all-games rate. The league rate comes from the league table directly, not from pattern rows.
- Ties: strength > goals > patterns, then the favourite's side (a winless/unbeaten mirror is told about the stronger team). No card when nothing scores above 0.
- A shortlisted favourite rides along as `big_dog`, so any winning card can carry the badge.
- Evidence (Oct 5 export, 560 fixtures): raw gap gave patterns 81% of cards; the shrunk scale splits goals 226 / patterns 325 / strength 9 before the strength fallback fix. Zlin v Slavia becomes Slavia Praha · Big dog (+35.9 vs Slavia scores 2+ at +31.2 × 0.76 = +23.7).
